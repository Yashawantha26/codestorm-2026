# Challenge 01 – Website Building Challenge
## Official Rules and Regulations

**Event:** CODESTORM 2026 – November Edition  
**Organizer:** GDG on Campus – RIT Hassan  
**College:** Rajeev Institute of Technology, Hassan  
**Department:** Computer Science and Engineering

## 1. Participation Rules

1. Participation is open to eligible students as announced by the organizers.
2. Participants may register individually or as a team, subject to the official team-size guidelines.
3. Every participant or team must complete the registration process.
4. Team participants must identify one representative for official communication.
5. Participants must provide accurate registration information.
6. Participants must follow all instructions announced by the organizers.

## 2. Project Development Rules

1. Participants must develop a website based on a meaningful idea or real-world purpose.
2. The project must satisfy the mandatory requirements specified in the problem statement and requirements document.
3. All three official logos must be included.
4. The website must contain the required sections and at least three meaningful features.
5. The website must be responsive and publicly accessible.
6. Participants must maintain a public GitHub repository containing the project source code.
7. Participants may use any suitable programming language, framework, or technology.

## 3. Originality and Academic Integrity

1. Participants are expected to submit original work.
2. Copying another participant's complete project and submitting it as original work is prohibited.
3. Existing templates and open-source libraries are permitted if they are appropriately customized and credited.
4. Participants must not claim ownership of work created entirely by someone else.
5. External images, libraries, APIs, and other resources must follow their applicable licenses.
6. Participants may be asked to explain their implementation.

## 4. AI Tool Usage

1. AI tools may be used for learning, brainstorming, debugging, and development assistance.
2. Participants are responsible for understanding the code generated or suggested by AI tools.
3. AI-generated content must be reviewed and tested before submission.
4. Participants must not use AI tools to impersonate another participant or misrepresent project ownership.
5. Organizers or judges may ask participants to explain AI-assisted parts of their projects.

## 5. Design and Content Rules

1. Websites must have appropriate and professional content.
2. Offensive, misleading, harmful, or illegal content is prohibited.
3. Participants must use images, icons, and other assets responsibly.
4. Official logos must not be distorted or unnecessarily modified.
5. Websites should provide a safe and user-friendly experience.

## 6. Submission Rules

1. Participants must submit the required project details through the official submission method announced by the organizers.
2. A public GitHub repository link is mandatory.
3. A live deployed website URL is mandatory.
4. Submitted links must be accessible to the evaluation team.
5. Participants must ensure that the submitted project is complete and functional.
6. The submission deadline will be announced officially.
7. Late submissions may be handled according to the instructions announced by the organizers.

**Important:** Event registration and project submission are separate processes unless the organizers explicitly announce otherwise.

## 7. Evaluation Rules

1. Projects will be evaluated according to the published evaluation criteria.
2. Judges may inspect the source code and deployed website.
3. Participants may be asked questions about their projects.
4. Evaluation will consider design, functionality, technical implementation, creativity, responsiveness, code quality, and presentation.
5. Mandatory requirements will be checked before final evaluation.
6. Evaluation decisions will follow the process communicated by the organizers.

## 8. Disqualification Conditions

A participant or team may be disqualified for:

- Submitting copied projects as original work.
- Providing false registration or project information.
- Violating the event's mandatory requirements.
- Submitting harmful, offensive, or illegal content.
- Attempting to manipulate the evaluation process.
- Engaging in unfair practices or misconduct.
- Violating official event instructions.

The organizers reserve the right to review reported violations.

## 9. Code of Conduct

All participants are expected to:

- Maintain respectful communication.
- Treat other participants and organizers professionally.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and disruptive behavior.
- Follow the event instructions.
- Maintain a collaborative and positive environment.

## 10. Organizer Authority

1. The organizers may clarify or update event instructions when necessary.
2. Any official updates will be communicated through the event's official channels.
3. Participants are responsible for checking announcements.
4. Decisions regarding rule interpretation and event administration will be handled by the organizers.
