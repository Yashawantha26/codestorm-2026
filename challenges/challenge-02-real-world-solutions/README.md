## 🚀 About the Challenge

Every community faces real-world problems that affect people's daily lives. Technology, creativity, and innovation can help develop meaningful solutions to these challenges.

In this challenge, participants are invited to identify a genuine real-world problem and propose an innovative, practical, and technology-driven solution.

The goal is to transform ideas into solutions that create positive changes in society.

## 🎯 Challenge Objective

Identify a real-world problem, understand its impact, and develop a creative solution that addresses it effectively.

Participants should demonstrate:

- Problem-solving ability.
- Innovation and creative thinking.
- Technical knowledge.
- Practical implementation.
- Social awareness and impact.

## 💡 Suggested Problem Domains

Participants can select problems from any domain.

| Domain | Example Problem Areas |
|---|---|
| 🎓 Education | Learning accessibility, student management, career guidance |
| 🌾 Agriculture | Crop monitoring, market information, irrigation management |
| 🏥 Healthcare | Health awareness, appointment management, accessibility |
| 🌱 Environment | Waste management, water conservation, pollution |
| 🚦 Transportation | Traffic management, road safety, parking |
| 💻 Technology | Digital literacy, online safety, accessibility |
| 🤝 Social Impact | Community support, public services, inclusion |

These are suggested domains. Participants are welcome to explore other meaningful real-world problems.

## 🛠️ What Participants Must Do

1. Identify a genuine real-world problem.
2. Explain the problem and its causes.
3. Identify the people affected by it.
4. Research existing approaches and limitations.
5. Propose an innovative solution.
6. Explain how the solution will work.
7. Identify suitable technologies or methodologies.
8. Demonstrate the expected benefits and impact.

## 📋 Solution Requirements

Every proposed solution should explain:

- **Problem Identification:** What problem are you addressing?
- **Target Users:** Who experiences this problem?
- **Root Cause:** Why does this problem occur?
- **Proposed Solution:** How will your idea solve it?
- **Technology:** Which tools or technologies will you use?
- **Implementation:** How can the solution be developed?
- **Feasibility:** Is the solution practical and achievable?
- **Social Impact:** What positive changes can it create?

## 💻 Suggested Technologies

Participants are free to choose suitable technologies.

**Web Development:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js.

**Mobile Development:** Flutter, Android, React Native.

**Programming:** Python, Java, C++, JavaScript.

**AI & Machine Learning:** Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn.

**Database:** MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Firebase, Supabase.

**Hardware & IoT:** Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, Sensors.

Technology selection is flexible. Focus on solving the problem rather than using unnecessary technologies.

## 📦 Expected Deliverables

Participants should prepare:

1. Problem statement.
2. Problem analysis and target users.
3. Proposed solution.
4. Implementation approach.
5. Technologies or methodology used.
6. Prototype, working model, design, or demonstration, as appropriate.
7. Expected benefits and social impact.

The organizers will announce the final submission format and additional requirements separately.

## 📜 Rules & Guidelines

- Original ideas and independent research are encouraged.
- Copying another participant's complete project is prohibited.
- AI tools may be used responsibly.
- Participants must understand their proposed solutions.
- External resources must be appropriately credited.
- Do not fabricate research results or implementation claims.
- Respect privacy and use personal data only with appropriate permission.
- Harmful or illegal project content is not permitted.
- Follow the official event submission instructions.
