# Challenge 02 – Real-World Problem & Solution
## Official Problem Statement

**Event:** CODESTORM 2026 – November Edition  
**Organizer:** GDG on Campus – RIT Hassan  
**College:** Rajeev Institute of Technology, Hassan  
**Department:** Computer Science and Engineering

## 1. Introduction

Every community faces real-world challenges that affect people's daily lives. Technology and innovation can play an important role in identifying these problems and developing practical solutions.

In this challenge, participants must identify a genuine real-world problem and propose an innovative, practical, and technology-driven solution.

The goal is not just to develop an idea but to demonstrate how technology can help solve a meaningful problem.

## 2. Main Challenge

**Identify a real-world problem and design a solution that addresses it using technology, innovation, or a creative approach.**

Participants must:

1. Identify a meaningful problem.
2. Understand who is affected by the problem.
3. Analyze the causes and challenges.
4. Propose an innovative solution.
5. Explain how the solution works.
6. Demonstrate its practical feasibility.
7. Present its expected benefits and impact.

## 3. Suggested Problem Domains

Participants may select problems from any domain.

### 3.1 Education
- Difficulty accessing quality learning resources.
- Student attendance and academic tracking.
- Career guidance and skill development.
- Improving communication between students and institutions.

### 3.2 Agriculture
- Helping farmers access market information.
- Crop disease identification.
- Water management and irrigation.
- Access to government schemes and agricultural guidance.

### 3.3 Healthcare
- Improving access to basic healthcare information.
- Appointment and patient management.
- Health awareness and preventive care.
- Connecting people with healthcare services.

### 3.4 Environment
- Waste management.
- Water conservation.
- Air pollution awareness.
- Sustainable living and recycling.

### 3.5 Transportation
- Traffic management.
- Public transportation accessibility.
- Road safety awareness.
- Parking management.

### 3.6 Technology and Society
- Digital literacy.
- Online safety awareness.
- Accessibility for people with disabilities.
- Community support platforms.
- Improving access to public information.

These domains are suggestions. Participants may identify problems in other areas as well.

## 4. Solution Requirements

Every proposed solution should clearly explain:

- **Problem Identification:** What is the actual problem?
- **Target Users:** Who experiences this problem?
- **Root Cause:** Why does this problem occur?
- **Proposed Solution:** How will your idea address it?
- **Technology:** Which technologies or methods will be used?
- **Implementation:** How can the solution be developed?
- **Feasibility:** Can the solution realistically be implemented?
- **Impact:** What positive changes can it create?

## 5. Expected Deliverables

Participants must prepare:

1. A clearly defined real-world problem.
2. A detailed explanation of the proposed solution.
3. A description of the target users.
4. A suitable implementation approach.
5. A technology stack or methodology, where applicable.
6. A prototype, working model, design, or demonstration, as appropriate.
7. An explanation of expected benefits and impact.

The organizers will announce the final submission format and any additional requirements.

## 6. Suggested Technologies

Participants may use suitable technologies based on their proposed solutions.

**Web Development:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js.

**Mobile Development:** Flutter, Android, React Native.

**Programming:** Python, Java, C++, JavaScript.

**AI and Machine Learning:** Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn.

**Database:** MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Firebase, Supabase.

**Hardware and IoT:** Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, sensors.

Technology selection is flexible. A strong solution should focus on addressing the problem effectively rather than using unnecessary technologies.

## 7. Innovation and Practical Impact

Participants should focus on solutions that are:

- Innovative and creative.
- Practical and achievable.
- Affordable and accessible.
- User-friendly.
- Scalable where appropriate.
- Useful to individuals or communities.

Solutions should explain their potential benefits and limitations honestly.

## 8. Evaluation

Projects will be evaluated based on the following proposed criteria:

| Evaluation Category | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Problem Identification | 15 |
| Innovation and Creativity | 20 |
| Practical Feasibility | 20 |
| Technical Implementation | 20 |
| Social Impact | 15 |
| Project Presentation | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

The final evaluation process will be communicated by the organizers.

## 9. Important Instructions

- Select a genuine and clearly defined problem.
- Avoid proposing solutions without explaining the actual problem.
- Original ideas and independent research are encouraged.
- AI tools and open-source resources may be used responsibly.
- Give credit to external sources and resources.
- Do not submit copied ideas or projects as entirely original work.
- Explain the practical implementation of your solution.
- Follow the official submission instructions and deadline.
