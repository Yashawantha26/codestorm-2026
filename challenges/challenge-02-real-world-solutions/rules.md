# Challenge 02 – Real-World Problem & Solution
## Official Rules and Regulations

**Event:** CODESTORM 2026 – November Edition  
**Organizer:** GDG on Campus – RIT Hassan  
**College:** Rajeev Institute of Technology, Hassan  
**Department:** Computer Science and Engineering

## 1. Participation Rules

1. Participation is open to eligible students as announced by the organizers.
2. Participants may participate individually or as a team, subject to the official team-size guidelines.
3. Registration must be completed through the official registration process.
4. Team participants must identify one representative for official communication.
5. Participants must provide accurate information.
6. All participants must follow the official event announcements and instructions.

## 2. Problem Identification Rules

1. Participants must identify a genuine real-world problem.
2. The selected problem should have a clear purpose and meaningful impact.
3. Participants must explain who is affected by the problem.
4. The causes and challenges associated with the problem should be clearly described.
5. Participants may select problems from any domain.
6. Participants are encouraged to conduct basic research before proposing a solution.

## 3. Solution Development Rules

1. Every solution must directly address the identified problem.
2. The proposed solution should be practical and understandable.
3. Participants must explain how their solution works.
4. Suitable technologies and methodologies may be selected freely.
5. A prototype, working model, design, or demonstration may be prepared as appropriate.
6. Participants should explain the feasibility, limitations, and expected benefits of their solutions.

## 4. Originality and Innovation

1. Original thinking and creativity are encouraged.
2. Copying another participant's complete project or presentation and claiming it as original work is prohibited.
3. Existing solutions may be studied for research and inspiration.
4. Participants should clearly explain what is new or improved in their proposed solution.
5. External research, images, datasets, libraries, and other resources must be appropriately credited.
6. Participants must not misrepresent another person's work as their own.

## 5. Responsible Use of AI Tools

1. AI tools may be used for brainstorming, research assistance, coding, analysis, and presentation preparation.
2. Participants are responsible for verifying AI-generated information.
3. Participants must understand the ideas, code, and technical decisions included in their submissions.
4. AI tools must not be used to fabricate research results, user feedback, or implementation claims.
5. Participants may be asked to explain AI-assisted work.

## 6. Technical and Safety Guidelines

1. Participants may use suitable programming languages, frameworks, databases, APIs, and hardware.
2. All external tools and services must be used responsibly.
3. Projects must not contain malicious code or harmful functionality.
4. Participants must respect privacy and obtain appropriate permission before using personal data.
5. Solutions involving health, finance, safety, or other sensitive areas must clearly communicate their limitations.
6. Participants must follow applicable licenses and usage conditions for external resources.

## 7. Submission Rules

1. Participants must prepare their problem statement and proposed solution clearly.
2. Submissions must follow the official format announced by the organizers.
3. Required project details and supporting materials must be provided.
4. Prototype links, source code, screenshots, or demonstration videos may be requested depending on the solution.
5. All submitted links must be accessible to the evaluation team.
6. The official submission deadline will be announced separately.
7. Late submissions will be handled according to the instructions communicated by the organizers.

**Important:** Event registration and project submission are separate processes unless the organizers officially announce otherwise.

## 8. Evaluation Rules

Projects will be evaluated based on the following proposed criteria:

| Evaluation Category | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Problem Identification | 15 |
| Innovation and Creativity | 20 |
| Practical Feasibility | 20 |
| Technical Implementation | 20 |
| Social Impact | 15 |
| Project Presentation | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Judges may ask questions about the identified problem, proposed solution, implementation, feasibility, and expected impact.

## 9. Disqualification Conditions

A participant or team may be disqualified for:

- Submitting copied work as original.
- Providing false or misleading information.
- Fabricating research, testing, or implementation results.
- Submitting harmful or illegal project content.
- Violating event rules or official instructions.
- Attempting to manipulate the evaluation process.
- Engaging in unfair practices or misconduct.

Reported violations will be reviewed by the organizers.

## 10. Code of Conduct

All participants must:

- Respect fellow participants, organizers, and judges.
- Maintain professional communication.
- Avoid harassment and discriminatory behavior.
- Follow event instructions.
- Maintain a positive and collaborative environment.

## 11. Organizer Authority

1. Organizers may clarify rules and issue additional instructions when necessary.
2. Official updates will be communicated through event channels.
3. Participants are responsible for checking announcements.
4. Decisions concerning event administration and rule interpretation will be handled by the organizers.
