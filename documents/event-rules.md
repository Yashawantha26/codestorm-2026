# 📋 CODESTORM 2026 – Rules and Regulations

### November Edition | GDG on Campus – RIT Hassan

---

## 1. General Rules

1. Participation is open to eligible students as announced by the organizers.
2. Participants must complete the official registration process.
3. Participants can take part individually or as a team.
4. Every participant must provide accurate registration details.
5. Participants must follow the instructions announced by the organizing team.
6. All submissions must be made through the official submission method.

## 2. Participation Guidelines

- Participants may select either Challenge 01 or Challenge 02, subject to event instructions.
- Individual and team participation are permitted.
- Team size, if applicable, will be announced by the organizers.
- Each team must identify one representative for communication.
- Participants should actively contribute to their projects.

## 3. Project Development Rules

1. Projects must be original and developed for the selected challenge.
2. Copying another participant's complete project is prohibited.
3. Existing templates and open-source libraries may be used with appropriate customization and attribution.
4. AI tools may be used responsibly.
5. Participants must understand the code and technologies used in their projects.
6. Third-party images, libraries, APIs, and resources must follow their respective licenses.
7. Projects must not contain malicious code or harmful functionality.

## 4. Challenge-Specific Rules

### Challenge 01 – Website Building Challenge

- Build a professional and responsive website.
- Include the required college, department, and GDG club logos.
- Implement at least three meaningful features.
- Ensure navigation and important links work correctly.
- Submit the GitHub repository and deployed website URL.
- Follow all mandatory requirements in the Challenge 01 documentation.

### Challenge 02 – Real-World Problem & Solution

- Identify a genuine real-world problem.
- Explain the problem clearly.
- Propose an innovative and practical solution.
- Describe the technologies used.
- Demonstrate the solution through a prototype, presentation, or project where required.
- Explain the expected benefits and impact.

## 5. Submission Rules

1. Submit projects before the officially announced deadline.
2. Provide correct participant and team details.
3. Include all required project links and documentation.
4. Ensure submitted GitHub repositories and live demonstrations are accessible.
5. Late submissions will be handled according to organizer instructions.
6. Incomplete submissions may affect evaluation.

## 6. Code of Conduct

All participants must:

- Maintain respectful communication.
- Respect fellow participants, judges, and organizers.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and inappropriate behavior.
- Maintain academic integrity.
- Follow a fair and collaborative competition environment.

## 7. Disqualification

A participant or team may be disqualified for:

- Plagiarism or copying another participant's work.
- Providing false registration information.
- Submitting harmful or inappropriate content.
- Manipulating evaluation or engaging in unfair practices.
- Violating important event rules.
- Failing to meet mandatory challenge requirements.

## 8. Evaluation and Judging

- Projects will be evaluated according to the published challenge-specific criteria.
- Judges may ask questions about project implementation.
- Participants should be able to explain their ideas, technologies, and development process.
- Judges' decisions will be final, subject to the event's announced evaluation process.

## 9. Organizer Authority

The organizing team reserves the right to:

- Update event instructions when necessary.
- Communicate changes through official channels.
- Reject submissions that violate event rules.
- Make decisions regarding event administration and fair participation.

## 10. Acceptance of Rules

By registering for CODESTORM 2026, participants agree to follow the official event rules, challenge requirements, and instructions communicated by the organizing team.
