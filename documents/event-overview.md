# 🚀 CODESTORM 2026 – November Edition

### GDG on Campus – Rajeev Institute of Technology, Hassan

**Theme: Think. Create. Innovate. Build the Future.**

---

## 1. Introduction

CODESTORM 2026 – November Edition is a technical event organized by Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDG) at Rajeev Institute of Technology, Hassan, under the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

The event aims to encourage students to explore technology, develop innovative ideas, strengthen programming skills, and create practical solutions to real-world problems.

CODESTORM provides a platform for students to demonstrate their creativity, technical knowledge, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities.

## 2. Event Objectives

- Encourage innovation and creative thinking.
- Improve website development skills.
- Promote real-world problem-solving.
- Develop practical technical knowledge.
- Encourage collaboration and teamwork.
- Introduce students to modern development technologies.
- Build confidence through project development and presentation.

## 4. Challenges

### Challenge 01 – Website Building Challenge

**Design • Develop • Deploy**

Participants must design and develop a professional, responsive, and interactive website based on a meaningful idea or purpose.

Expected skills:
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- React or Next.js
- UI/UX Design
- Backend Development
- Database Integration
- Website Deployment

### Challenge 02 – Real-World Problem & Solution

**Identify • Innovate • Solve**

Participants must identify a real-world problem and propose or develop a practical technology-based solution.

Suggested areas:
- Education
- Agriculture
- Healthcare
- Environment
- Transportation
- Social Impact
- Technology

## 5. Expected Outcomes

By participating in CODESTORM 2026, students will have opportunities to:

- Build practical projects.
- Improve technical and analytical skills.
- Develop creative solutions.
- Understand real-world technology applications.
- Improve communication and presentation skills.
- Gain experience working independently or in teams.
