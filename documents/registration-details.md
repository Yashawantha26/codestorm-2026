### 💻 Challenge 01 – Website Building Challenge

## 4. Registration Process

Follow these steps:

1. Open the official registration form.
2. Enter your name and required details.
3. Provide your college and department information.
4. Select your preferred challenge.
5. Enter team details if applicable.
6. Review the information carefully.
7. Submit the registration form.
8. Join the official November event WhatsApp group for updates.
