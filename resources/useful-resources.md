## 11. Project Development Tips

Follow these suggestions while building your project.

1. Understand the problem before starting development.
2. Plan your website structure and features.
3. Select suitable technologies.
4. Create a simple and professional design.
5. Build and test features step by step.
6. Make your website responsive.
7. Organize your source code properly.
8. Upload your project to GitHub.
9. Deploy your website.
10. Prepare a clear project explanation.

## 12. Important Guidelines for Participants

- These resources are suggestions, not mandatory tools.
- Participants are free to use other suitable learning and development platforms.
- Follow the licenses of third-party resources.
- Respect intellectual property and originality.
- Use AI tools responsibly.
- Focus on functionality, creativity, and practical value.
